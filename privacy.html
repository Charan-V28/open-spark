Privacy Notice

This is a working draft for product development, not a complete privacy policy. It must be reviewed by qualified privacy counsel and updated to match the actual service, vendors, jurisdictions, and data practices before public registration.

Information in this demo

If a Supabase project is configured, its authentication service processes the email address and password you submit to create or access an account. Launchpad does not receive or store the password itself. Supabase's terms and privacy notice also apply.

Saved opportunities and status choices are currently stored in this browser's local storage, scoped by account ID. They are not synced to a server or across devices. Study text and generated examples are held in memory in your browser tab and are not uploaded or sent to an AI service.

Shared devices and deletion

People with access to your browser profile may be able to see saved items. Use the account privacy control to clear local saved data and sign out. Clearing browser storage does not delete an account from Supabase; account deletion is not implemented in this demo.

Security and future launch

Do not upload sensitive, identifying, school-confidential, health, financial, or other private information. Before public use with minors or school information, Launchpad needs a full legal/privacy review, data retention and deletion workflows, vendor agreements, server-side authorization, and an incident response process.

Questions

A public operator name, contact address, retention schedule, legal bases, jurisdiction-specific rights, and complete vendor list must be added before launch.
